# NVDA — Calendar Q2 2025 (fiscal Q2 FY2026, quarter ended July 27, 2025)

Source: Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended July 27, 2025 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]. All figures below are **GAAP** — a 10-Q provides no non-GAAP basis [assessment].

## Headline results (GAAP)

- Revenue: $46,743M, up 56% YoY (vs $30,040M) and up 6% QoQ (vs $44,062M) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Gross margin (GAAP): 72.4% (up 11.9 pts QoQ, down 2.7 pts YoY). This source provides GAAP only; no non-GAAP gross margin is stated in the 10-Q [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Operating income: $28,440M (vs $18,642M YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Net income: $26,422M (vs $16,599M YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Diluted EPS: $1.08 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Operating cash flow (first half FY2026): $42,779M [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities: $56.8B at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]

## Geographic revenue (by customer billing location)

United States $23,470M; Singapore $10,156M (22%; invoicing hub — over 99% of controlled Data Center compute revenue billed to Singapore was for US-based customer orders); Taiwan $8,529M; China (incl. Hong Kong) $2,769M; Other $1,819M [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf].

## H20 status this quarter

- Recognized approximately $650M of H20 revenue from sales to an unrestricted customer outside China, with a $180M release of previously reserved H20 inventory; no H20 sales to China-based customers in the quarter [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- In August 2025 (subsequent to quarter end), the USG granted licenses allowing shipment of certain H20 products to certain China-based customers; no revenue generated or shipments made under those licenses as of the filing [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- USG officials expressed an expectation of receiving 15% of revenue from licensed H20 sales, not yet codified in regulation [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]

## Products / drivers

- [[wiki/products/blackwell]] grew 17% sequentially, including transition to [[wiki/products/blackwell-ultra]]; production units of Blackwell Ultra platforms including GB300 began shipping during the quarter [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Large CSPs represented approximately 50% of Data Center revenue [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Networking growth driven by NVLink compute fabric for GB200/GB300 systems, XDR InfiniBand ramp, and Ethernet-for-AI adoption [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]

## Capital return

- Repurchased 67 million shares for $9.7B in the quarter ($24.2B / 193M shares in 1H); $14.7B authorization remaining at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Subsequent events: repurchased 20M shares for $3.5B (Jul 28–Aug 26, 2025); on August 26, 2025 the Board approved an additional $60.0B repurchase authorization without expiration, bringing total availability to $71.2B [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Dividends paid: $244M ($0.01/share) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]

## Investments & commitments

- OBBBA enacted July 2025; tax effects of certain provisions recognized, no material impact on the quarter [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
