# NVDA — Calendar Q1 2025 (fiscal Q1 FY2026, quarter ended April 27, 2025)

Source: Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended April 27, 2025 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]. All figures below are **GAAP** — a 10-Q provides no non-GAAP basis [assessment].

- Revenue: $44,062M, up 69% YoY (vs $26,044M) and up 12% QoQ (vs $39,331M in fiscal Q4 FY2025) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

- Net income: $18,775M (+26% YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Diluted EPS: $0.76 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Net cash provided by operating activities: $27,414M (vs $15,345M YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities: $53.7B at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

## H20 export-control charge (defining event of the quarter)

- On April 9, 2025, the USG informed NVIDIA that a license is required for export of H20 integrated circuits (and any circuits matching H20 memory/interconnect bandwidth) to China (incl. Hong Kong/Macau) and D:5 countries [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Resulting charge of $4.5B in the quarter for H20 excess inventory and purchase obligations ($1.9B inventory provision + $2.6B excess purchase obligation charges); charge was less than initially anticipated because certain materials could be re-used [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- H20 sales were $4.6B in the quarter prior to the new licensing requirements [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- The charge was the primary driver of the YoY gross-margin decline from 78.4% to 60.5%, along with the initial ramp of more sophisticated Data Center systems [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

## Products / drivers

- [[wiki/products/blackwell]] ramp extended beyond large CSPs to consumer internet companies and regional CSPs; large CSPs remained just under 50% of Data Center revenue [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Blackwell Ultra expected to begin shipping samples and production units in fiscal Q2 2026 (see [[wiki/products/blackwell-ultra]]) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Networking growth driven by NVLink compute fabric in GB200 systems and Ethernet-for-AI adoption [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- CoreWeave investment reclassified from non-marketable to marketable equity securities following its IPO; fair value ~$1B at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

## Capital return

- Repurchased 126 million shares for $14.5B during the quarter; $24.3B authorization remaining at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Subsequent repurchases Apr 28–May 23, 2025: 19 million shares for $2.3B under a pre-established trading plan [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Dividends paid: $244M ($0.01/share) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

## Investments & commitments

- Inventory purchase and long-term supply/capacity obligations of $29.8B, up YoY, led by commitments for the Blackwell architecture [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Other non-inventory purchase obligations of $13.7B, including $10.6B of multi-year cloud service agreements (primarily supporting R&D and DGX Cloud) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]

## M&A

No material M&A events. Two small acquisitions closed in March 2025; acquisitions net of cash acquired were $383M and goodwill increased $310M (Compute & Networking unit) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]. Immaterial tuck-ins rather than a material M&A boundary [assessment].
