# NVDA — Calendar Q3 2025 (fiscal Q3 FY2026, quarter ended October 26, 2025)

Source: Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended October 26, 2025 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]. All figures below are **GAAP** — a 10-Q provides no non-GAAP basis [assessment].

## Headline results (GAAP)

- Revenue: $57,006M, up 62% YoY (vs $35,082M) and up 22% QoQ (vs $46,743M) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Gross margin (GAAP): 73.4% (up 1.0 pt QoQ, down 1.2 pts YoY). This source provides GAAP only; no non-GAAP gross margin is stated in the 10-Q [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Operating income: $36,010M (+65% YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Net income: $31,910M (+65% YoY) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Diluted EPS: $1.30 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Operating cash flow (first nine months FY2026): $66,530M [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities: $60.6B at period end [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## Geographic revenue — basis changed this quarter

Beginning this quarter, geographic revenue is reported by **customer headquarters location** (previously billing location); prior periods recast [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]:

- United States $39,177M; Taiwan $13,751M; China (incl. Hong Kong) $2,973M; Other $1,105M [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- An estimated 86% of Data Center revenue from Taiwan-headquartered customers is attributed to end customers based in the US and Europe [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## H20 status this quarter

- Approximately $50M of H20 revenue generated to date under the August 2025 USG licenses for China-based customers; H20 sales were insignificant in the quarter [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## Products / drivers

- [[wiki/products/blackwell-ultra]] is now the leading architecture across all customer categories, while prior-generation [[wiki/products/blackwell]] saw continued strong demand; Blackwell architectures are the majority of Data Center revenue [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Networking growth driven by NVLink compute fabric for GB200/GB300 systems, XDR InfiniBand growth, and Ethernet-for-AI adoption [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## Strategic investments

- Investment commitments of $6.5B as of period end, including $5B in Intel Corporation subject to regulatory approval [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Letter of intent entered in fiscal Q3 FY2026 with an opportunity to invest in OpenAI [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- In November 2025 (subsequent to quarter end), agreement signed, subject to closing conditions, to invest up to $10B in Anthropic [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Facility lease guarantee entered in the quarter: max gross exposure $860M over five years, partner escrow of $470M, warrants received in exchange [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## Capital return

- Repurchased 70 million shares for $12.6B during the quarter ($36.7B / 262M shares in first nine months); $62.2B authorization remaining at period end after the August 26, 2025 additional $60B authorization [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Subsequent repurchases Oct 27–Nov 14, 2025: 6 million shares for $1.1B [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Dividends paid: $243M ($0.01/share) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]

## Balance sheet / commitments

- Short-term debt of $999M appeared on the balance sheet (3.20% Notes due 2026 now within one year); long-term debt $7,468M [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Manufacturing/long-term supply and capacity commitments of $50.3B, substantially all payable through FY2027 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Multi-year cloud service agreement commitments of $26B [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
