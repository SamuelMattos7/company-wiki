# NVDA — Calendar Q1 2026 (fiscal Q1 FY2027, quarter ended April 26, 2026)

Source: Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended April 26, 2026 [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]. All figures below are **GAAP** — a 10-Q provides no non-GAAP basis [assessment].

## Headline results (GAAP)

- Revenue: $81,615M, up 85% YoY (vs $44,062M) and up 20% QoQ (vs $68,127M in fiscal Q4 FY2026) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Gross margin (GAAP): 74.9% (vs 60.5% YoY; ~flat vs 75.0% QoQ). This source provides GAAP only; no non-GAAP gross margin is stated in the 10-Q [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Operating income: $53,536M (vs $21,638M YoY) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Net income: $58,321M (vs $18,775M YoY) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Diluted EPS: $2.39 (vs $0.76 YoY) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Net cash provided by operating activities: $50,344M (vs $27,414M YoY) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities: $50.3B at period end [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## Market platforms (new presentation this quarter; prior periods recast)

- Data Center: $75,246M, +92% YoY, +21% QoQ [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
  - Hyperscale: $37,869M [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
  - AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise: $37,377M [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## Geographic revenue (by customer HQ)

United States $63,769M; Taiwan $12,006M; China (incl. Hong Kong) $4,550M; Other $1,290M [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]. Sales to customers headquartered outside the US were 22% of revenue vs 42% a year ago [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf].

## Drivers & commentary

- Growth driven by data center products for accelerated computing and AI solutions; Blackwell accounted for the majority of system shipments, and the Blackwell 300 ramp plus InfiniBand, Spectrum-X Ethernet, and NVLink demand drove Data Center growth [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Gross margin up YoY primarily due to the prior year's $4.5B H20 excess inventory/purchase obligation charge; net inventory-provision impact on gross margin was an unfavorable 1.2 pts this quarter vs 11.0 pts a year ago [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Edge Computing growth driven by Blackwell workstation demand, partially offset by slower consumer PC demand tempered by elevated memory and systems prices [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Other income, net of $16,367M included $13.4B unrealized gains on publicly-held equity securities and $2.6B on non-marketable equity securities [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Effective tax rate: 16.6% (vs 14.3% YoY), increase driven primarily by a lower percentage of stock-based compensation tax benefits relative to pre-tax income growth [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## Capital return

- Repurchased 108 million shares for $20.2B during the quarter; $38.5B remained authorized as of April 26, 2026 [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- On May 18, 2026 (subsequent to quarter end), the Board approved an additional $80.0B repurchase authorization without expiration [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Dividends paid: $243M ($0.01/share); on May 18, 2026 the quarterly dividend was raised from $0.01 to $0.25 per share, payable June 26, 2026 [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## Investments & commitments

- Invested $18.6B in private companies and infrastructure funds during the quarter, some in AI model makers that may indirectly purchase or use NVIDIA products [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Manufacturing/supply/capacity commitments of $119B as of April 26, 2026 ($95B payable in the remainder of FY2027) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Multi-year cloud service agreement commitments of $30B, primarily to support R&D [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Accrued purchase consideration of $3,957M related to the Groq, Inc. non-exclusive license agreement [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## M&A

No material M&A events this quarter. Two small acquisitions closed (Feb 17, 2026 and Apr 10, 2026); combined acquisitions net of cash acquired were $87M and goodwill increased only $62M [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]. These are immaterial tuck-ins rather than a material M&A boundary [assessment].

## Export controls / China

- No Data Center Hopper shipments to China occurred during the quarter, vs $4.6B in fiscal Q1 FY2026 [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- Beginning February 2026, USG licenses permit small amounts of H200 shipments to specific China-based customers; no revenue generated under the program as of the filing; units must pass US inspection and carry a 25% tariff upon importation into the US [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
- NVIDIA states it remains effectively foreclosed from China's data center compute market under current export controls [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]
