# H20

China-market data center GPU designed to comply with US export controls. Status `eol` reflects that NVIDIA was effectively foreclosed from China's data center compute market by the end of the covered period; small licensed sales continued into calendar Q3 2025 [assessment].

## Timeline

- Calendar Q1 2025 (fiscal Q1 FY2026): $4.6B of H20 sales before April 9, 2025 USG license requirement; resulting $4.5B charge for excess inventory and purchase obligations ($1.9B inventory provision + $2.6B purchase-obligation charges) [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- Calendar Q2 2025 (fiscal Q2 FY2026): ~$650M sold to an unrestricted customer outside China with a $180M provision release; no China-based customer sales; August 2025 USG licenses granted for certain China shipments but unused as of the filing [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250727.pdf]
- Calendar Q3 2025 (fiscal Q3 FY2026): ~$50M cumulative revenue under licenses; sales insignificant [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
- Calendar Q1 2026 (fiscal Q1 FY2027): no Data Center Hopper products shipped to China during the quarter (vs $4.6B a year earlier) [direct, source: raw/nvda-20260426.pdf]

## Notes

- The April 2025 license requirement covers H20 and any circuits matching its memory bandwidth, interconnect bandwidth, or combination thereof [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf]
- USG officials expressed an expectation of receiving 15% or more of revenue from licensed H20 sales; never codified in regulation [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20251026.pdf]
