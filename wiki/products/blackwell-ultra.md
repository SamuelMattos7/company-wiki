Successor platform within the Blackwell family. First referenced in this repo's sources in [[wiki/quarters/nvda-2025-q1]], when NVIDIA said it expected to begin shipping samples and production units in fiscal Q2 2026 [direct, source: raw/fillings/nvda-20250427.pdf].
